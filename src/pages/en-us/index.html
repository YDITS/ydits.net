About YDITS

Quickly provides users with the optimal disaster prevention information

YDITS (pronounced [waɪ dɪts]) is a disaster prevention service that develops applications and other services with the target of quickly providing users with the optimal disaster prevention information.

Contribute to society in the form of disaster prevention

We all experience earthquake, tsunami, volcanic eruption, and other disasters in our daily lives, and disaster prevention information is indispensable in times of disaster.
YDITS operator 'Yone' wants to contribute to society in the form of disaster prevention.
